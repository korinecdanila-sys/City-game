# Production roadmap — proposal

1. Finish preproduction: specifications, engine comparison, art reference, asset and budget audit, acceptance criteria.
2. Owner gives explicit permission to begin programming.
3. Minimal technical slice: one small environment, input/camera, scene transition, one interaction, clock and save; install and profile on target Android device.
4. Expand technical slice: off-screen simulation, dialogue, investigation, weather, UI, transport.
5. Art slice: consistent character, environment, animations and performance tiers.
6. Closed test, accessibility and compatibility QA.
7. Early access and planned expansion.

The technical slice is not the intended first full release. No delivery dates, guaranteed scale or guaranteed frame rates before testing. Do not silently cut approved scope; raise budget and schedule conflicts.
