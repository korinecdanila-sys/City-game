# Project brief

A narrative third-person 3D RPG for landscape Android, combining investigation, relationships and a persistent simulated world. Other platforms are subject to technical review.

Public-safe design summary:
- Explore large connected districts with loading boundaries and accessible interiors.
- Context-sensitive movement and cinematic interactions; optional first-person camera, gamepad and configurable touch controls.
- Time, weather, NPC schedules and persistent world state.
- Branching investigations and consequences, character development, daily activities and economy.
- Cinematic QTE rather than free-form combat; configurable HUD and difficulty.
- Localization-ready content, scalable graphics and target 30/60 FPS modes (not performance guarantees).
- Closed tests, early access and expansion.

Production constraints: minimum budget, licensed assets with art direction, owner approval of new design decisions, no coding until the explicit post-preproduction command. Final art direction and engine selection remain open. This brief intentionally excludes story spoilers and private references.
