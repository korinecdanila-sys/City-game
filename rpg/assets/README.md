# Asset production and license tracking

Place only cleared assets here after approval. Categories: models, materials, textures, animation, audio and UI.

Every third-party or AI-generated asset needs source/provider, author, source URL, original license or usage terms, commercial and redistribution permissions, modifications, review status and approval date. Do not assume a free download implies permission to redistribute source files. Keep private references outside Git. Large binary storage and quotas must be reviewed before importing assets.
